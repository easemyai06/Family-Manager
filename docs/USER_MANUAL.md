# ❤ FamilyHome — User Manual

Everything your family needs in one warm, private place — calendars, chores, chat, memories, wish lists, emergency info and more. This guide walks through every screen.

> A polished, printable version is available at **`user-manual.html`** (open it in any browser and use *Print → Save as PDF*). Screens use a sample "Sharma Family"; your app shows your own family.

---

## 1. Getting started
![Welcome](manual/00_welcome.png)

- **Get Started** — create a new family space; you become the organizer.
- **Continue with Google** — sign in with Google.
- **I already have an account** — sign in with email & password.

One person creates the family; everyone else *joins* with an invite link or code (and can claim a profile you've pre-made for them).

## 2. Signing in
![Login](manual/01_login.png)

- **Email/username & password**, then **Sign In**.
- **Forgot password?** → we email a **6-digit code**; enter it with a new password.
- **Family member? Sign in with a PIN** → tap your saved photo and type a 4-digit PIN.

![Forgot](manual/02_forgot.png) ![PIN](manual/03_pin.png)

## 3. The Home screen
![Home](manual/10_home.png)

Your daily glance: family **status strip**, header **search / customize / 🔔 bell**, **Needs your attention** (SOS, chores, shopping, expiring documents, birthdays) and **Today at a glance**. Bottom bar: Home · Calendar · Family · Chat · More.

## 4. Activity & notifications
![Activity](manual/11_notifications.png)

Tap the **🔔 bell** for one tidy inbox of family activity — new posts, memories, messages, chores done, love sent, events, notes and upcoming birthdays. New items show a red count; tap to jump straight there.

## 5. Calendar & events
![Calendar](manual/13_calendar.png)

Add events with the **+** button — time, place, people, **repeat** rules and **RSVPs**. Share to other calendars with a standard calendar file.

## 6. Family & members
![Family](manual/14_family.png)

Members are **Joined** or **Pending**. Organizer/parents can tap a member to change role, set up/reset a **login & PIN**, resend an invite, or remove them. Invite people from **More → Invite Family**.

## 7. Kids' logins & PINs
Children need no email. On the **Family** tab, tap a child → **Set up login & PIN** → choose a username and 4-digit PIN. Kids then sign in via **Family member? Sign in with a PIN**. Tap **Reset login & PIN** anytime. Only parents/organizer can manage a child's login.

## 8. Your profile
![Member](manual/12_member.png)

Tap your photo on Home (or a member on the Family tab) to view a profile — photo, role, birthday. Parents can edit member details.

## 9. Family Chat
![Chat](manual/15_chat.png)

One simple group conversation. Send text, photos, **voice notes** and quick ❤ love; see read receipts and reply to messages. SOS alerts appear here too.

## 10. Send some love
![Love](manual/16_affection.png)

**More → Send Some Love** — send a hug, kiss, high-five or note to one person or everyone.

## 11. Chores & rewards
![Chores](manual/17_chores.png) ![Rewards](manual/18_rewards.png)

Set jobs for each child; they tick them off, earn **stars**, build streaks and badges, and appear on the **Family Rewards** leaderboard.

## 12. Shopping, meals & recipes
![Shopping](manual/19_shopping.png) ![Meals](manual/20_meals.png) ![Recipes](manual/21_recipes.png)

Shared **Shopping** and **To-Do** lists, a weekly **Meal Planner**, and **Recipes** that auto-add ingredients to the shopping list.

## 13. Wish lists & gifts
![Wishlist](manual/22_wishlist.png)

Everyone gets a gift wish list. Family can quietly **reserve** gifts (hidden from the list's owner) to avoid duplicates.

## 14. Memories & family tree
![Timeline](manual/23_timeline.png) ![Tree](manual/24_tree.png)

**Our Family Story** timeline (with On This Day), **Albums**, **Places We've Been**, **Weekly Highlights**, **Time Capsules**, and a **Family Tree**.

## 15. Family Vault
![Vault](manual/25_vault.png)

A private, **PIN-protected** place for important documents. Organise into folders, set who can see each item, and get reminders before things expire.

## 16. Emergency Center & SOS
![Emergency](manual/26_emergency.png)

**Medical at a glance** (blood group + allergies; detailed medical info stays private to the person, parents and trusted contacts), **emergency contacts**, a family plan, **trusted access** for a relative, and one-tap **SOS** (with optional location) that alerts everyone and posts to Family Chat.

## 17. Settings & accessibility
![Accessibility](manual/27_accessibility.png)

**More → Accessibility & Display**: text size (up to Extra Large), high contrast, reduced motion, bigger buttons, **Grandparent mode** (big & simple) and **Kids mode**. Themes and notification preferences are in More too.

## 18. Account, PIN & your data
![Account](manual/28_account.png)

**More → Account & Data**: set/change/remove your **Quick Sign-In PIN**, view/link sign-in methods, export family data (organizer), or delete your account.

### The "More" menu
![More](manual/29_more.png)

Everything that isn't a main tab lives here — love, chores, lists, meals, recipes, memories, wish lists, rewards, vault, emergency center, invites, profile and settings.
