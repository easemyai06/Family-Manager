Chapter 1

Getting started

When you first open FamilyHome you'll land on the welcome screen. From here you can:

  • Get Started — create a brand-new family space. You'll pick a family name and become the family organizer.
  • Continue with Google — sign in quickly with your Google account.
  • I already have an account — sign in with your email and password.

Once your family exists, invite everyone else with a shareable invite link or a short invite code (see “Family & members”). New members can also claim a profile you've already created for them, so nothing gets duplicated.

Tip: One person creates the family and becomes the organizer. Everyone else joins — they don't create their own family.

Chapter 2

Signing in

Email & password

Enter your email (or username, for kids) and password, then tap Sign In.

Forgot your password?

Tap Forgot password? on the login screen. We'll email you a 6-digit code. Enter the code plus a new password and you're back in — no waiting.

Quick sign-in with a PIN

Tap Family member? Sign in with a PIN to see the faces saved on this device. Tap your photo and type your 4-digit PIN. Grown-ups set their PIN in Account → Quick Sign-In; kids get theirs from a parent.

Good to know: PINs only work on a phone where someone has already signed in once, so your family stays protected on new devices.

Chapter 3

The Home screen

Home is your family's daily glance. At the top you'll find:

  • Family status strip — everyone's photo; tap your own to set a status (e.g. “At work”, “Free later”).
  • Search, Customize and the 🔔 bell (Activity) in the header.
  • Needs your attention — the important stuff today: an active SOS, chores left, items to buy, documents expiring soon, upcoming birthdays.
  • Today at a glance — the day's events.

The bottom bar switches between Home, Calendar, Family, Chat and More.

Home dashboard
Chapter 4

Activity & notifications

Tap the 🔔 bell on Home to open your Activity inbox — one tidy list of everything happening in the family:

  • New posts, memories and messages
  • Chores your kids finished and love that was sent
  • Events and notes others added
  • Upcoming birthdays

Anything new shows a red dot and a count on the bell. Tap any item to jump straight to it. Pull down to refresh.

Chapter 5

Calendar & events

The Calendar tab shows the family's plans. Tap a day to see events, or use the + button to add one.

  • Add a title, date/time, location and who's involved.
  • Make it repeat (daily, weekly, monthly) for things like classes or bin day.
  • Invite people and collect RSVPs — see who's coming at a glance.
  • Share an event to other calendars with a standard calendar file.

Chapter 6

Family & members

The Family tab lists everyone. Members are either Joined (they've signed in) or Pending (a profile waiting to be claimed).

Tap a member (organizer/parents only) to:

  • Change their role (parent, adult, child)
  • Set up or reset a login & PIN — great for kids (see next chapter)
  • Re-send an invite (via WhatsApp or a share link)
  • Remove a member

Invite new people from More → Invite Family using the link or code.

Chapter 7

Kids' logins & PINs

Children don't need their own email. A parent can give each child a simple login:

  • Go to the Family tab and tap a child.
  • Choose Set up login & PIN.
  • Pick a username and a 4-digit PIN (a password is optional).

Now your child can sign in by tapping Family member? Sign in with a PIN, choosing their photo and typing their PIN. Need to change it later? Open the same screen and tap Reset login & PIN any time.

Safety: Only parents and the organizer can manage a child's login, and no one can turn a member into an organizer this way.

Chapter 9

Family Chat

Tap the Chat tab to open your family's single group conversation — simple and in one place. You can:

  • Send text, photos and voice notes (tap the mic)
  • Send a quick ❤ love tap
  • See who's read messages and reply to specific ones

Emergency SOS alerts also appear here so everyone sees them instantly.

Chapter 15

Family Vault

More → Family Vault is a private, PIN-protected place for important documents — insurance, IDs, warranties, school papers. The first time you open it you'll create a 4-digit Vault PIN.

  • Organise documents into folders.
  • Set who can see each item (whole family, parents only, or specific people).
  • Get gentle reminders before things like insurance expire.
Vault PIN screen
Chapter 16

Emergency Center & SOS

The Emergency Center (More → Emergency Center) keeps everything you'd need in a crisis:

  • Medical at a glance — blood group and allergies for each person (detailed medical info stays private to that person, parents and trusted contacts).
  • Emergency contacts and a family plan.
  • Trusted access — let an adult relative view key info if needed.
  • SOS — send an instant alert (with your location, if you allow it) to the whole family; it appears in Family Chat.

Chapter 17

Settings & accessibility

More → Accessibility & Display makes the app comfortable for everyone:

  • Text size — up to Extra Large.
  • High contrast and reduced motion.
  • Bigger buttons and labels with icons.
  • Grandparent mode — a big, simple Home with just the essentials.
  • Kids mode — a friendly Home focused on a child's day.

You can also pick a theme and manage notification preferences from the More menu.

Chapter 18

Account, PIN & your data

More → Account & Data is where you manage your own sign-in and data:

  • Quick Sign-In — set, change or remove your 4-digit unlock PIN.
  • See and link your sign-in methods (email, Google, Apple).
  • The organizer can export the family's data.
  • Delete your account and data whenever you choose.
Account & Data

The “More” menu

Everything that isn't a main tab lives under More — send love, chores, lists, meals, recipes, memories, wish lists, rewards, the vault, the emergency center, invites, your profile and all settings.
